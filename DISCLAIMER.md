# Disclaimer

The data provided by this MCP fleet is **informational only** and does not constitute legal advice. It is not a substitute for reading the official regulations published by each Swedish agency.

## Data Sources

Regulations are scraped from the official websites of five Swedish agencies: Arbetsmiljoverket (AV), Boverket, MSB, Folkhalsomyndigheten (FoHM), and Stralsakerhetsmyndigheten (SSM). Swedish agency regulations are published under PSI (Public Sector Information) and open data re-use rules.

## Accuracy

While we make reasonable efforts to keep the data current and accurate, there may be delays between when an agency publishes an update and when it appears in the database. Regulations may also be amended, repealed, or reissued between ingest cycles.

**Always verify against the official source** before relying on any provision for compliance, legal, or safety decisions. Links to the official source are included in every tool response via the `_citation` field.

## SIS Standards

Some regulations reference standards published by the Swedish Institute for Standards (SIS). These standards are copyrighted and are **not included** in the database. Where a regulation references an SIS standard, the database contains a `reference_only` stub with the standard number and title. The full text must be purchased from SIS.
