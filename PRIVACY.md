# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection

This MCP fleet does **not** collect, store, or process any personal data. The servers are read-only databases of publicly available Swedish regulations.

- No cookies are set.
- No user tracking or analytics.
- No IP address logging beyond standard HTTP transport.
- No authentication or user accounts.

## Query Data

Queries sent to the MCP servers are processed in-memory and are not persisted, logged, or transmitted to third parties.
